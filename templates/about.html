The Matrix is an American media franchise created by the Wachowskis — a landmark in science fiction that questioned the nature of reality itself. Beginning with The Matrix (1999), the series expanded into a full trilogy and eventually a fourth film, The Matrix Resurrections (2021). With its fusion of cyberpunk aesthetics, martial arts action, and deep philosophical themes drawn from Plato, Baudrillard, and Buddhist thought, The Matrix didn't just entertain — it changed how we think about technology, free will, and the world around us.
